Namma Space (Spatial Computing)
Abstract: Namma Space delivers an end-to-end spatial-computing pipeline converting consumer smartphone video into metrically accurate, browser-native 3D digital twins. Instead of coupling visual rendering to geometric navigation, the system establishes a Dual-Layer Spatial Architecture: a continuous 3D Gaussian Splatting radiance field providing photorealistic view-dependent appearance at 60 FPS, decoupled from a discrete polygon Navigation Mesh (NavMesh) enabling collision bounds, dynamic A* trajectory planning, and localized spatial commerce indexing for Juspay.
1. Deterministic Optical Capture SOP & Mathematical Quality Gate

A. Optical Acquisition Protocol

• Sensor: 4K UHD video (60 FPS, 1/120s shutter speed).
• Exposure Lock: Fixed manual exposure and 5600K white balance to eliminate photometric drift.
• Trajectory: Closed perimeter loop with 75–80% inter-frame visual overlap.

B. Frame Filtering Metric

Frames are evaluated using the variance of the discrete image Laplacian to reject motion-blurred captures prior to pose estimation:

Bk = Var(Δ Ik) ≥ τblur

Frames failing τblur or exhibiting luminance clipping (τsat) are pruned automatically.

C. Environmental Controls

• Lighting: Consistent artificial ambient illumination.
• Multi-Elevation: Passes at +30° (overhead structural loft) and -25° (floor plane).
• Decoupled Capture: High-density video for geometry; static frames for detail.

2. Photometric Reconstruction & Optimization Formulations

Structure-from-Motion (SfM) Bundle Adjustment

Joint non-linear optimization of camera extrinsics (Ri, ti) and 3D landmark points Xj minimizes reprojection error over visible image keypoints xij under robust Huber loss ρ:

min{Ri, ti, Xj} ∑i,j ρ( || xij - π(Ri Xj + ti) ||2 )

3D Gaussian Splatting Covariance & Radiance

Each volumetric splat is parameterized by position μ, opacity α, spherical harmonic coefficients, and 3D covariance matrix Σ factorized into rotation R and scaling S:

Σ = R S ST RT,   C = ∑i=1N ci αi ∏j=1i-1 (1 - αj)

4. NavMesh Graph & Collision-Free A* Trajectory Planning

Modified A* Graph Search Formulation

Navigation computes optimal traversal on the dual graph of traversable NavMesh polygons using admissible Euclidean distance heuristics:

f(n) = g(n) + h(n),   h(n) = || xn - xgoal ||2

Node expansion considers turning cost penalties and clearance margins (≥ 0.45m) around obstacles (beds, workstation, cabinet walls) to ensure human walkability.

Trajectory Smoothing & Spline Interpolation

Waypoints extracted from the discrete polyline path are smoothed into continuous $C^1$-continuous curves using centripetal Catmull-Rom splines:

p(u) = ∑i=03 bi(u) pi,   u ∈ [0, 1]

Generates an animated floor waypoint ribbon that guides users seamlessly between room coordinates.

5. Juspay Spatial Commerce: Micro-Merchant & Desk-Node Integration

Juspay’s participation in Namma Space is anchored in expanding digital commerce into physical spatial coordinates. In the digital twin, every Point of Interest (e.g., Workstation Alpha, Study Pods, Campus Storefronts) functions as an addressable Spatial Merchant Node. The runtime architecture integrates:

1. Proximity Geofencing

Detects user arrival within 1.2m of a workstation node, triggering ambient payment intent.

2. HyperSDK Checkout

In-engine modal supporting 1-click UPI, cards, and campus smart wallet transactions.

3. Proof-of-Presence

Cryptographic token generated upon successful coordinate verification for door/desk access.
