Executive Abstract: Traditional indoor 2D architectural blueprints fail to convey spatial awareness, visual immersion, and obstacle-aware human navigability. Namma Space solves this by pioneering an end-to-end spatial pipeline transforming standard consumer smartphone captures into interactive, browser-native 3D digital twins. Combining camera pose estimation, structural indoor geometry extraction, real-time collision boundaries, spatial Point-of-Interest (POI) indexing, and dynamic A* pathfinding splines, this solution provides a robust infrastructure for remote campus exploration, commercial facility management, and future AR on-site navigation.
Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Indoor Capture Protocol

1. Optical & Sensor Setup

• Sensor: 4K video capture at 60 FPS (1/120s shutter speed).
• Exposure Lock: Fixed manual exposure and 5600K white balance to avoid photometric drift.
• Stabilization: Hardware OIS enabled with steady 2-handed chest-level rig.

2. Volumetric Trajectory

• Trajectory: Closed-loop perimeter walk with circular return to establish loop closure.
• Overhead Coverage: Angled sweeps at +30° (loft shelf) and -25° (floor grid).
• Overlap: Continuous 75–80% visual overlap between sequential keyframes.

3. Environmental Control

• Lighting: Constant artificial illumination; elimination of dynamic window glare.
• Static Geometry: Zero human/object movement in frame during capture.
• Keyframe Extraction: Sharpness-filtered sampling at 3 fps via FFmpeg.

Physical Space Digital Twin: Model Geometry & Semantics

Spatial Geometry & Boundaries

• Bounding Volume: 4.8m (W) × 3.2m (H) × 5.4m (L) volumetric envelope.
• Floor Grid: Large-format polished ceramic tiles with soft specular reflection.
• Structural Loft (Chhajja): Reinforced concrete cantilever shelf positioned at Y=2.2m with storage box props.
• Egress Boundary: Dark chocolate flush-panel door with steel security bolts.

Interior Furnishings & Compute Node

• Workstation Alpha: Dark wenge wood laminate desk with side storage tower.
• Display Unit: 27" widescreen display with active telemetry dashboard.
• Input Devices: White mechanical keyboard, optical mouse, and laptop.
• Twin Bed Cots: Symmetrically arranged resting cots (East: Teal, West: Lilac).

Interactive Navigation Architecture & Performance

Dual-Mode Camera System

• First-Person Roaming: Pointer-lock mouse-look with clamped pitch (-85° to +85°), WASD movement, and continuous collision avoidance with room walls.
• Dollhouse / Orbit: Elevated architectural cutaway view with orbital trajectory for macroscopic space inspection.

Top-Down 2D Spatial Radar (Minimap)

• Live Coordinate Synchronization: Real-time mapping of user (X, Z) positions onto a top-down floorplan canvas.
• Dynamic Field-of-View Cone: Visualizes the user's active viewing direction and yaw rotation relative to room assets.
